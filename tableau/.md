# 🚗 Car Sales Dashboard – Data Analytics Case Study

## Project Overview  
Built a dynamic and interactive Car Sales Dashboard to analyze dealership performance using Tableau.  
The dashboard provides real-time insights into sales trends, pricing patterns, and regional performance, helping stakeholders make data-driven decisions.

---

## Objective  
The goal of this project was to:
- Track sales performance over time  
- Analyze pricing trends  
- Identify top-performing regions and car categories  
- Enable interactive exploration using filters  

---

## Key Features & KPIs  

### Sales Performance
- YTD Total Sales  
- Year-over-Year (YoY) Growth in Sales  

### Pricing Analysis
- YTD Average Price  
- YoY Growth in Average Price  

### Volume Metrics
- YTD Cars Sold  
- YoY Growth in Cars Sold  

---

## Dashboard Visualizations  

### Weekly Sales Trend
Line chart showing sales fluctuations over time. Helps identify seasonality and demand spikes.

### Sales by Body Style
Pie chart showing distribution across SUV, Sedan, Hatchback, etc.

### Sales by Color
Donut chart showing customer color preferences.

### Sales by Dealer Region
Bar chart comparing regional sales performance.

### Company-wise Sales Table
Tabular grid displaying:
- Company name  
- Cars sold  
- Average price  
- Total sales  
- Contribution percentage

---

## Business Insights  
- Sales showed consistent growth with peak spikes in later weeks  
- SUVs contributed the highest share of total sales  
- Pale white and black were the most preferred colors  
- Certain regions significantly outperformed others  
- Average price remained relatively stable
